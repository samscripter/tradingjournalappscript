Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Trading Journal add-on for Google Sheets™ ("the Add-on") handles information. The Add-on is provided by Trading Journal. All rights reserved ("we", "us", or "our").

What the Add-on accesses

The Add-on operates within Google Sheets™ and may access information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • The Google Sheets™ document where the Add-on is installed and used.
  • CSV files that you choose to upload through the Add-on.
  • Trading records contained in those CSV files for the purpose of importing them into your spreadsheet.

How data is used

The Add-on uses the information you provide solely to import and organize your trading records within your Google Sheets™ trading journal. We do not use your trading data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Data storage

The Add-on does not maintain a separate external database of your trading records. CSV data is processed as necessary to perform the import operation and the resulting information is stored in the Google Sheets™ document you use.

Third-party services

The Add-on operates within Google Sheets™ and uses Google services required to provide its functionality. The Add-on does not send your trading data to external third-party servers.

Data retention

The Add-on does not maintain a separate copy of your trading data. Imported trading records remain in your Google Sheets™ document until you choose to modify or delete them.

Your control over your data

You control the trading information stored in your Google Sheets™ document. You can modify or delete your records at any time. You can also uninstall the Add-on from your Google account.

Security

The Add-on relies on Google Sheets™ and Google's security mechanisms for access to your spreadsheet. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Google account and spreadsheet access.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Add-on, please contact us at papafx4@gmail.com.
